Tesla Roadster wall flip: simulation

A physics simulation and Blender render of the wall-flip stunt in the PCMag video quRC56Z0EJw, 1:41–2:00. These videos are renders of the simulation, not real footage.

Project document (measurements, model, change log) Interactive 3D replay Source code

Broadcast cut with HUD and sound

13.97 s, 1280x720, EEVEE draft. Sim time 44.0–57.98 s. G-FORCE and ALTITUDE gauges show sim values with the measured 0.30 s HUD lag. Sound effects: ElevenLabs, mixed to the sim's nozzle commands and wall speed.

Broadcast cut, no HUD

13.97 s, 1280x720, EEVEE draft. Same edit as the broadcast feed: camera A (long lens behind the wall foot) and camera B (tripod).

Coverage cut (extra angles)

20.5 s, 1280x720, EEVEE draft. Cameras that did not exist at the event: C low wide at the foot, D on the truss above the lip, E on the car's right flank, F chase drone (0.6 s lag), G low across the landing zone, plus a 0.4× replay of the release.
